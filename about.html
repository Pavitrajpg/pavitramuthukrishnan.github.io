About

Turning Complexity into Clarity

My career has never followed a single industry - it has followed a single purpose: solving problems.

From healthcare marketing and media production to retail leadership and international education, every role has strengthened my ability to understand businesses, build relationships, and execute solutions that create value.

I believe successful account management goes beyond maintaining relationships. It requires understanding business goals, aligning stakeholders, anticipating challenges, and delivering consistent results.

Today, I bring together operational thinking, strategic communication, and AI-enabled workflows to help teams work smarter and businesses grow sustainably.
